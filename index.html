Methodology

Hourly revenue exposure = company annual gross income ÷ selected annual operating hours. Productivity = employees × hourly labor cost × outage hours × productivity loss %. Revenue = revenue at risk per hour × outage hours × revenue lost %. Recovery = engineers × recovery hours × recovery hourly cost. Gross = productivity + revenue + recovery + other incident costs + penalties. Net = gross − expected SLA credit, floored at zero.

SLA credits are subject to contract terms. Avoid double-counting labor productivity and lost revenue. This calculator is a planning estimate, not an official provider SLA determination.
